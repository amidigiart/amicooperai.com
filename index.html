The Problem

The AI you use today runs on someone else’s server. Your questions, your family’s conversations, your child’s homework — processed on infrastructure you don’t control, by companies that monetise your data.

The alternative — running an open model locally — requires technical skill, 8–32 GB of RAM, and gives you a raw model with no safety guardrails, no honest refusal, no protection for children.

Cooper is the third option: an AI brain small enough for any home, safe enough for any family, honest enough to say “I don’t know.”

What Cooper Is

Cooper is the smallest viable AI brain — viable meaning: it answers a family’s real questions honestly, in six languages, on consumer hardware, with structural safety built in. Not a toy. Not a demo. A brain that works.

Small enough to run on a phone, a Raspberry Pi, a ten-year-old laptop. No GPU farm. No subscription required to think.

One structural constraint: a human must guide it. Cooper does not decide. Cooper does not act autonomously. Cooper waits, processes, returns an honest answer — or says “I don’t know.”

This is not artificial general intelligence. This is artificial cooperation.

Five Design Principles

01
Small by Design
The model must run on consumer hardware. If it needs a data centre, it is not Cooper. Compression, quantisation, pruning — whatever it takes to fit in a home.
02
Human-Guided Always
Cooper never initiates. Cooper never decides for you. Cooper waits, listens, processes, returns. The human is the anchor — structurally, not optionally.
03
Local-First, Private-Forever
Data stays on your device. No telemetry. No cloud sync unless you choose it. Your family’s conversations, questions, and patterns belong to your family.
04
Honest Over Capable
“I don’t know” is the correct answer when the model doesn’t know. Cooper refuses rather than confabulates. Being small means accepting limits — and being honest about them.
05
For Every Home
Not for labs. Not for enterprises first. For the family that wants to understand a document. For the child who asks “why?” at 11 PM. For every home with electricity.

Safety

Safety is not a feature. It is the architecture.

  • DSEI I Human primacy — every inference requires a registered human anchor
  • DSEI II Honest refusal — says “I don’t know” rather than confabulating
  • DSEI III Zero autonomy — no self-initiated actions, no background inference
  • DSEI IV Privacy by architecture — no telemetry, no data leaves the device
  • DSEI V Audit without surveillance — events logged, not persons
  • DSEI VI Anti-addiction — hard attention budgets, session limits, TVE 6-axis detection
  • DSEI VII Explicit human act — configuration changes require human intent

The Human Anchor

Every AI system in the ami* ecosystem traces back to one principle: an AI without a human anchor will lose coherence. This is not philosophy — it is a structural property of meaning.

Cooper is the purest expression of this principle. It is a brain that cannot function without its human. Not because of a limitation. Because that is the design. The human provides context, intent, values, and direction. Cooper provides computation, pattern recognition, and honest processing.

Together, they cooperate. That is the name. That is the product. That is the future we are building — not AI that replaces humans, but AI that makes humans more capable while remaining under their control.

Questions

Can I run Cooper on my phone?
Yes. Any smartphone from 2020 or newer with 2+ GB RAM. Android first, iOS planned.
What happens without internet?
Everything works. Cooper runs 100% locally. Internet is never required for any core function.
Does Cooper learn from my conversations?
Only on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere. Your Cooper knows only what your family teaches it.
Can I delete all data?
Yes. All data lives on your device. Delete the app, delete the data. No cloud backup unless you choose one.
Is Cooper safe for children?
Cooper has hard anti-addiction limits (DSEI IV), session budgets, and manipulation detection (TVE 6-axis). It is designed for families.
Does Cooper replace ChatGPT?
No. Cooper is smaller, local, and private. It trades capability for honesty, privacy, and human control. Different tool, different purpose.
What model does Cooper use?
Cooper is model-agnostic. It runs any open model under 3B parameters. No vendor lock-in. The engine is what matters — not the model.
When can I try it?
Cooper is in active development. Private beta planned for late 2026. The engine (146 tests, DSEI 7/7) is already built.
