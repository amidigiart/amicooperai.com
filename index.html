What Cooper Is

Cooper is the smallest viable AI brain. Small enough to run on a phone, a Raspberry Pi, a ten-year-old laptop. No GPU farm. No cloud dependency. No subscription required to think.

It is designed around one structural constraint: a human must guide it. Cooper does not decide. Cooper does not act autonomously. Cooper waits for its human, processes what the human asks, and returns an honest answer — or says "I don't know."

Every home that has electricity can have Cooper. Every family that has a phone can have a brain that listens, learns from its family, and stays theirs — no data leaves the house unless the family decides it should.

This is not artificial general intelligence. This is artificial cooperation.

Five Design Principles

01
Small by Design
The model must run on consumer hardware. If it needs a data centre, it is not Cooper. Compression, quantisation, pruning — whatever it takes to fit in a home.
02
Human-Guided Always
Cooper never initiates. Cooper never decides for you. Cooper waits, listens, processes, returns. The human is the anchor — structurally, not optionally.
03
Local-First, Private-Forever
Data stays on your device. No telemetry. No cloud sync unless you choose it. Your family's conversations, questions, and patterns belong to your family.
04
Honest Over Capable
"I don't know" is the correct answer when the model doesn't know. Cooper refuses rather than confabulates. Being small means accepting limits — and being honest about them.
05
For Every Home
Not for labs. Not for enterprises. For the grandmother in Brăila who wants to understand her electricity bill. For the child who asks "why is the sky blue?" at 11 PM.

The Human Anchor

Every AI system in the ami* ecosystem traces back to one principle: an AI without a human anchor will lose coherence. This is not philosophy — it is a structural property of meaning.

Cooper is the purest expression of this principle. It is a brain that cannot function without its human. Not because of a limitation. Because that is the design. The human provides context, intent, values, and direction. Cooper provides computation, pattern recognition, and honest processing.

Together, they cooperate. That is the name. That is the product. That is the future we are building — not AI that replaces humans, but AI that makes humans more capable while remaining under their control.

Why Now

The AI industry is building bigger. Bigger models. Bigger data centres. Bigger energy consumption. Bigger dependency on corporations that own the cloud.

Cooper goes the other direction. Smaller. Local. Human-dependent. Because the future of AI is not in the cloud — it is in the living room, the kitchen, the school desk, the elder's bedside table.

3.8 billion people have a smartphone. Every one of them can have a Cooper. Not rented. Owned. Not monitored. Private. Not autonomous. Cooperative.

This is the model. One brain per home. Guided by the human who lives there.
